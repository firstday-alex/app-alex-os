Tidy-ups

Housekeeping so nothing gets forgotten: tickets missing a ROCK, a due date or an owner, ROCK values with no rock, and anything you marked fixed that is still true (marked ↺). Oldest first. Dismiss hides one for today; Never show again hides it for good.

Store metrics

Store-number moves from the latest readout. The full numbers live in the Ecom Reporting Hub; AB test results are in AB Testing. Both are linked here.

Daily summary

The short version to post: the top wins with who to thank, what is in progress, what other teams have asked for (your ECOM list and their ClickUp comments), and the metrics worth a look. Built from the wins on 4. Speak, the sprint, Slack and the readout; edit it, then copy.

Start here

The few things across the whole app that need you now, most important first. Each one opens the page where you deal with it.

Ecom Rocks

Every active rock and the tactics under it, with their owners, from the latest readout. A rock's ring is its course (red off course, amber drifting, green on course); a tactic's ring is its status, and the dark dot counts its open tickets.

Workshop

The rock workshop agenda: every rock × tactic, and a rock update where work sits on a rock with no tactic. What's had work in last week's or this week's sprint is on the agenda, most important first (Workshop Priority tactics, anything off track or overdue, a rock off course, then what there is to show). Anything with nothing done or moving since Monday of last week is grayed below. Skip or Discuss anyway to change it; Copy pastes the agenda.

Rocks

Planning by month: each rock with the sprint and backlog tickets that ladder up to it through its Big Swing. Set a target month on each rock to place it. Every change is recorded with what it was before.

Quarter plan

The quarter by month and week. Each rock, the tactics under it, the work you plan for each week, and the ClickUp tickets that landed or are due in it. Plan a week with +. The course check up top reads the evidence, not the status in the standup.

1:1

Everything about the person you are meeting: their rocks, their sprint and what each ticket waits on, what is queued for them, open flags, and talking points to copy into your notes.

Sprint Management

The week's commitment: every open ticket, how long it has sat in its status, what it is waiting on, and the last thing said on it in ClickUp.

Ticket Hygiene

Fields to set and hands to check, each fixed in ClickUp from here: open sprint work with no ROCK or a due date to fix, and tickets done this sprint that changed hands or are missing a ROCK or TACTIC.

Backlog

What is queued for the next sprint, kept apart from the sprint itself: every open backlog ticket by rock, then coverage of the urgent and high tickets by person and by rock.

Update + Stand-up

The daily update with each person's latest stand-up from #ecomm-team since the start of the previous working day. A stand-up line sits under a ticket when it clearly names one of that person's tickets (shared words, or an acronym like FBB); a ticket someone mentioned is added even if ClickUp was not touched. Everything else is under "Also from stand-up", then blockers. Untick anything that should not go in.

Kudos pages

Every kudos page you saved with Save version on This week's wins or Last week's wins, by week, newest first. Each keeps its own copy of its wins and images, so it opens the same later. Open previews it; Download gives the .html, named after the version.

Starred wins

The wins you graded A+, A or A- on Post/Speak › 4. Speak, kept for good. Grouped by the week or the month the work was completed, newest first, A+ first within each. Starring a win does not take it off the wins list; only Called out does.

This week's wins

Every ticket in "done (this sprint)", summed up by the ClickUp ROCK field, then kudos by person. Use it for a mid-week or Friday shout-out; untick anything that should not go in, then copy.

Last week's wins

Every finished ticket on the Last Sprint list (last sprint's work, status "complete"), summed up by the ClickUp ROCK field, then kudos by person. Each line is the ticket names ClickUp holds; untick anything that should not go in, then copy.

Stand-up

What each person posted in #ecomm-team: yesterday, today and blockers. Read live from Slack each time this opens; nothing is stored.

My stand-up

Your Yesterday / Today / Blockers for #ecomm-team, drafted from your ClickUp activity, your Slack messages, your notes, your last stand-up and what you marked Waiting on. Written after the 8 AM readout; edit it here, copy, and post it yourself. Every line shows what it came from.

Speak: wins, in progress, asks

What to say at stand-up, one line per point: the top wins and who to thank, the work furthest along, and what other teams have asked for. The top five in each section show; the rest fold. Tick Called out once you've said a win; anything not ticked carries over to tomorrow. Full detail has every tag, the why in full and the ECOM list quick actions. Written after the 8 AM readout, or when you press the button; every figure is checked against the data.

Ideation

Every ticket in the current sprint (everything but complete): successful, unsuccessful or too early to call, the root cause of why, and three other ways to approach it. Read from each ticket's description and comments in ClickUp, the last week of stand-ups in Slack, the Intelligems tests and the rocks. Runs only when you press the button, as one call to Claude.

Overall Impact

Everything Alex and the team have shipped, in themes, each item linked to the work: tickets done, tests launched and concluded, rocks shipped, and days building this system. It grows each working morning at 9 and gets regrouped as it grows. Nothing is ever removed; an item that loses its theme goes to "Not yet sorted".

Lifetime value references

What a customer is worth over the horizon, by how they were acquired. Every experiment's future-value projection rests on these two numbers; without them the projection is withheld rather than assumed.
