Readout

Tests ready for a call

Intelligems tests that reached Ship, Iterate or Kill with no decision written in their notes. Use it to make the call.

Every open flag

The full list behind your queue. Use it to filter, group or triage many at once.

Rocks at a glance

Every leadership rock with its owner, state and last mini readout. Use it to check nothing is ownerless or gone quiet.

Who has rock work

Each person's sprint split into tickets on a rock and business as usual. Use it to spot anyone with no rock work.

Tidy-ups

Minor ClickUp housekeeping, kept out of your queue so it never escalates. Clear it when you have a spare few minutes.

Noisy rules

Which flag rules you keep ignoring, and one click to move them to Tidy-ups. Check it every couple of weeks.

Quarter plan

        
        
      

      
The quarter by month and week. Each rock, the tactics under it, the work you plan for each week, and the ClickUp tickets that landed or are due in it. Plan a week with +. The course check up top reads the evidence, not the status in the standup.

      

      

      

    


    

      
1:1

      
Everything about the person you are meeting: their rocks, their sprint and what each ticket waits on, what is queued for them, open flags, and talking points to copy into your notes.

      

      

    


    

      
Sprint

      
The week's commitment: every open ticket, how long it has sat in its status, what it is waiting on, and the last thing said on it in ClickUp.

      

    


    
    

      
Backlog

      
What is queued for the next sprint, kept apart from the sprint itself: every open backlog ticket by rock, then coverage of the urgent and high tickets by person and by rock.

      

    


    
    
    

      
Update + Stand-up

      
The daily update with each person's latest stand-up from #ecomm-team since the start of the previous working day. A stand-up line sits under a ticket when it clearly names one of that person's tickets (shared words, or an acronym like FBB); a ticket someone mentioned is added even if ClickUp was not touched. Everything else is under "Also from stand-up", then blockers. Untick anything that should not go in.

      

    


    
    

      
This week's wins

      
Every ticket in "done (this sprint)", summed up by the ClickUp ROCK field, then kudos by person. Use it for a mid-week or Friday shout-out; untick anything that should not go in, then copy.

      

    


    

      
Last week's wins

      
Every ticket in "dont use" (formerly "complete": last sprint's work), summed up by the ClickUp ROCK field, then kudos by person. Each line is the ticket names ClickUp holds; untick anything that should not go in, then copy.

      

    


    
    

      
Stand-up

      
What each person posted in #ecomm-team: yesterday, today and blockers. Read live from Slack each time this opens; nothing is stored.

      

    


    
    

      
My stand-up

      
Your Yesterday / Today / Blockers for #ecomm-team, drafted from your ClickUp activity, your Slack messages, your notes, your last stand-up and what you marked Waiting on. Written after the 8 AM readout; edit it here, copy, and post it yourself. Every line shows what it came from.

      

    


    

      
Daily Speech

      
The day's storyline to give the team: one headline, the win, the stakes, today, and a line to close. Written from the latest readout, the last three days of stand-ups, what Overall Impact recorded as shipped this week, your notes from the day, and ClickUp comments from people outside the team. Add notes as things come in (a Slack ask, a meeting takeaway): each speech reads everything from midnight of the previous working day up to when you press the button, so Monday's reaches back to Friday. Every figure is checked against that data and any it can't find is flagged before you say it. Runs only when you press the button, as one call to Claude.

      

    


    
    

      
Ideation

      
Every ticket in the current sprint (everything but complete): successful, unsuccessful or too early to call, the root cause of why, and three other ways to approach it. Read from each ticket's description and comments in ClickUp, the last week of stand-ups in Slack, the Intelligems tests and the rocks. Runs only when you press the button, as one call to Claude.

      

    


    
    

      
Overall Impact

      
Everything Alex and the team have shipped, in themes, each item linked to the work: tickets done, tests launched and concluded, rocks shipped, and days building this system. It grows each working morning at 9 and gets regrouped as it grows. Nothing is ever removed; an item that loses its theme goes to "Not yet sorted".
