Sprint

      
The week's commitment: every open ticket, how long it has sat in its status, what it is waiting on, and the last thing said on it in ClickUp.

      

    


    
    

      
Update

      
Every sprint ticket touched since the start of the previous working day (updated, closed or commented on) that is to do, in progress, in QA or VQA, ready for deploy, or done (this sprint). Complete tickets are left out. Grouped by Big Swing, one per line. Untick anything that should not go in.

      

    


    
    

      
Accomplishments

      
Every sprint ticket in complete or done (this sprint), by the date it was done, grouped by Big Swing, newest first. Untick anything that should not go in.

      

    


    
    

      
Ideation

      
Every ticket in the current sprint (everything but complete): successful, unsuccessful or too early to call, the root cause of why, and three other ways to approach it. Read from each ticket's description and comments in ClickUp, the Intelligems tests and the rocks. Runs only when you press the button, as one call to Claude.
